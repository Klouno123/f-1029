🐶 About Paws & Tails

Our Mission: We believe that every pet deserves the very best. Paws & Tails was founded in 2025 by a team of animal lovers with a simple goal: to provide high-quality, safe, and fun products for pets and their owners.

Every product in our catalog—from kibble to collars—is hand-selected and tested for safety, quality, and wag-worthiness. We are passionate about animals and committed to providing an exceptional shopping experience.

Thank you for choosing us! 🐾❤️
